# Business context

The simulated workwear group operates acquired brands in separate Business
Central companies and sells through country-specific dealer webshops. Products
have size, colour and logo/embroidery attributes. Operations needs a single
controlled path from order capture to warehouse tracking without forcing a
company merge.

Success means that an order reaches the right legal entity once, operational
staff can distinguish bad input from a technical outage, and a warehouse result
is traceable back to the external order reference.
